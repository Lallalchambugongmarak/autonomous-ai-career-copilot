# Autonomous AI Career Copilot — Project Roadmap

> A progressive roadmap from AI Career Intelligence to a research-driven, self-evaluating and increasingly autonomous agentic AI platform.

---

## 1. Project Vision

**Autonomous AI Career Copilot** is being developed as a modular multi-agent AI platform that progressively evolves from career intelligence into a broader **Multi-Agent Research & Automation Platform**.

The long-term research direction is to develop increasingly autonomous systems capable of:

* reasoning
* research
* planning
* collaboration
* verification
* memory
* tool usage
* workflow automation
* self-evaluation
* self-improvement
* increasingly autonomous operation

The project follows a principle of **progressive autonomy**:

> Autonomy must be earned through capability, verification, evaluation and reliability.

# 18. Engineering Principles

The project will follow these principles throughout development:

### Modularity

Each major capability should remain independently understandable and testable.

### Specialization

Agents should have clear responsibilities instead of becoming one large undifferentiated agent.

### Shared Intelligence

Agents should be able to exchange validated information through controlled shared state.

### Verification

Important outputs should be checked before being trusted.

### Observability

Agent decisions, state transitions, failures and execution should be inspectable.

### Evaluation

New capabilities should be measured rather than assumed to work.

### Progressive Autonomy

The system should become more autonomous only as reliability improves.

### Safety

Execution and automation should include appropriate validation, permissions and failure controls.

# 20. Important Development Rule

This roadmap intentionally separates:

* ✅ Completed capabilities
* 🔄 Current development
* 🔮 Planned capabilities
* 🔬 Long-term research directions

Future capabilities must not be presented as implemented capabilities until they actually exist, are tested and can be demonstrated.

The goal is not to claim autonomy.

The goal is to **engineer, evaluate and progressively earn autonomy**.
